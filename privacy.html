Privacy

Microphone testing should feel private.

This policy explains what happens when you use TestYourMicrophone.com. The microphone tool is designed to process the live stream in your browser rather than upload it to our server.

Microphone access

The browser asks for microphone permission only after you click the test button. You can deny, revoke, or change this permission through your browser and operating system settings.

Audio processing and recordings

The live level meter, device details, and recordings are created locally in your browser. TestYourMicrophone.com does not send microphone audio to a server as part of the microphone test. A recording remains available in the page while it is open, and it is downloaded only when you choose the download link.

Local preferences

The page stores one optional preference in local browser storage: whether you want the interface to remember your “Autostart next time” choice. This preference never starts the microphone by itself; you must still click the test button and approve the browser prompt.

Basic website data

Like most websites, the hosting platform may process basic request information such as an IP address, browser type, and requested URL for security, delivery, and reliability. We do not need an account or personal profile for the microphone test.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the tool or its hosting changes. The effective version is the one published on this page.
