How to test your microphone

Five seconds to a useful answer.

You do not need an account or a download. Connect your microphone, allow access, and use the meter and short recording to check it before a Zoom, Teams, Meet, or Discord call.

Start the microphone test →
  1. 01

    Click the test button

    Start the test when you are ready to give this page microphone access.

  2. 02

    Allow microphone access

    Choose Allow in your browser prompt. The browser controls when a site can use your mic.

  3. 03

    Speak and watch the meter

    Say a few words at your normal volume. The level bars should move as you speak.

  4. 04

    Switch input devices if needed

    Use the device list to select your headset, USB microphone, or another available input.

  5. 05

    Record a short clip

    Play the local recording back to check how your voice actually sounds.

Microphone FAQs

How do I test my microphone?

Find quick answers for how to test a microphone online, on Windows 10 and 11, on a PC, Mac, laptop, Microsoft Teams, or with AirPods.

How to test microphone online?+

Click Start microphone test, allow microphone access when your browser asks, and speak normally. The live meter should move as your microphone picks up sound. You can also record a short clip and play it back locally. People searching for how test microphone or how to test my microphone usually want this quick live level check.

How do I test my microphone?+

Click Start microphone test, allow browser access, speak at a normal volume, and watch the live meter. If the meter responds, your microphone is receiving audio. Record a short clip if you also want to hear the playback.

How to test if microphone is working?+

Start the microphone test, allow browser access, and speak into the microphone. If the level meter responds and a recorded clip plays back clearly, the microphone is working. If there is no signal, check the selected input, mute switch, and device connection.

How to test microphone Windows 10?+

To test microphone Windows 10, open Settings, choose System, then Sound, and select your microphone under Input to check the input level. You can also use this online microphone test: click Start microphone test, allow access, speak, and confirm that the live meter responds.

How to test microphone Windows 11?+

To test microphone Windows 11, open Settings, go to System, then Sound, and choose your microphone under Input. This explains how to test microphone on Windows 11: use the input test to check the signal, or click Start microphone test on this page and speak to see the live level meter respond.

How to test microphone on Mac?+

To test microphone on Mac, open your Mac sound settings, select Sound, and choose the microphone under Input. Check that the input level changes when you speak. You can also use this page to allow browser access, watch the live meter, and play back a short local recording.

How to test microphone on laptop?+

Make sure the laptop microphone is not muted, click Start microphone test, and allow access in your browser. Speak at a normal volume and confirm that the level meter moves. If you use a headset or USB microphone, select it from the input device list.

How to test microphone in Teams?+

To test microphone in Teams, open Microsoft Teams Settings, choose Devices, select the microphone you want to use, and run the available test call or microphone check. This also answers how to test microphone on Teams. You can test the same microphone here first by checking the live meter and local recording.

How to test AirPod microphone?+

To test AirPod microphone input, connect your AirPods, select them as the input device in your computer or browser, and click Start microphone test. Speak normally and confirm that the meter responds. Record a short clip to check how the AirPods microphone sounds during a call.

How to test my AirPods microphone?+

To test AirPods microphone input, connect your AirPods, choose AirPods as the input device, and click Start microphone test. If you are searching how to test airpods microphone, speak normally and confirm that the live meter moves. A short local recording helps you check whether the AirPods microphone sounds clear.

How to test microphone on PC?+

Connect or enable the microphone on your PC, open this page, and click Start microphone test. Allow browser access, speak normally, and confirm that the live meter responds. Select the correct USB, headset, webcam, or built-in microphone if more than one input is available.

How to test microphone on Windows?+

To test microphone on Windows, open Settings, choose System, then Sound, and select the microphone under Input. For a quick browser check, click Start microphone test here, allow access, and speak until the live input meter responds.

How can I test my microphone?+

The easiest way to test your microphone is to click Start microphone test, allow access, speak at a normal volume, and watch the live meter. Record a short clip if you also want to hear your microphone playback.

Can I use an online microphone test for singing?+

Yes. If you are searching for an am I a good singer microphone test online, click Start microphone test, allow access, and sing at the volume you normally use. Watch the live meter for a consistent signal, then record a short clip and play it back to check clarity, volume, and distortion.

Why is my microphone not working?+

Check browser permission, the selected input device, the microphone mute switch, and the cable or wireless connection. Close other apps that may be using the microphone, then reload this page and try the test again.

Can I test my microphone without uploading audio?+

Yes. This microphone test processes the live stream in your browser. The test does not upload microphone audio to a server, and recordings made here remain local until you close the page or download a clip.
