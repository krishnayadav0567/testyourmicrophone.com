What the tool does

The site provides a browser-based microphone check with a live input meter, available input device list, basic stream details, and an optional local recording. Results can vary by browser, device, operating system, permissions, network environment, and audio hardware.

No guarantee of hardware performance

A moving meter confirms that the browser received an audio signal; it does not certify professional audio quality, driver health, frequency response, noise floor, or suitability for a particular meeting or recording workflow.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for choosing the correct microphone, granting permission, handling downloaded recordings, and following applicable laws when recording yourself or others. Do not use a recording feature to capture another person without appropriate consent.
