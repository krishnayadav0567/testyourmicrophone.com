Meeting Audio Setup

How to configure audio in Teams & Zoom

Key checks before starting meetings or video interviews.

01

Microsoft Teams Audio Check

Open Teams Settings > Devices. Under Audio devices, verify your headset or USB mic is selected. Click Make a test call to verify audio delivery.

02

Zoom Audio Settings

In Zoom, open Settings > Audio. Uncheck "Automatically adjust microphone volume" if your voice volume fluctuates or cuts out unexpectedly.

03

Google Meet Input Selection

In Google Meet before joining, click the Audio and video settings gear icon. Test your microphone and confirm the green audio waves respond when speaking.

Troubleshooting

Fix meeting microphone errors

Resolve the most common video conferencing sound issues.

1. App-Exclusive Lock

If another meeting app (like Skype, Slack, or Discord) is open in the background, it may lock your microphone exclusively. Close unused calling apps.

2. Headset Bluetooth Profiles

Bluetooth headphones like AirPods or Sony XM4/XM5 switch to low-bandwidth Hands-Free mode during calls. Select the headset as both input and output device.

3. Browser Permission Check

If using Teams or Zoom in a browser, click the lock / tune icon in the URL bar and ensure Microphone is toggled to Allow.

4. PowerPoint Live & Rehearsal

Before presenting in PowerPoint Live or Teams webinars, test your mic here to make sure speech-to-text live captions and presenter audio are crystal clear with zero clipping.
