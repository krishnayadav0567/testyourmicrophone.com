Mac Settings

How to configure microphone input on macOS

Configure input sensitivity and privacy on your Mac.

01

Mac Sound Settings

Go to System Settings > Sound > Input. Select your built-in mic or USB interface and adjust the Input volume slider so your normal speaking voice reaches 70-80%.

02

Safari Microphone Permissions

In Safari, open Settings > Websites > Microphone. Ensure testyourmicrophone.com is set to Allow instead of Deny or Ask.

03

Voice Isolation & Mic Modes

During FaceTime, Zoom, or web calls, click the Control Center icon in your Mac menu bar and choose Mic Mode > Voice Isolation to filter out background noise.

Troubleshooting

Fix Mac microphone problems

Quick solutions for when your Mac mic is quiet, silent, or blocked.

1. Privacy & Security Lock

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Make sure your browser has the toggle switched ON. Restart the browser if prompted.

2. Low Input Sensitivity

If others say you sound quiet, go to System Settings > Sound > Input and increase Input volume. Make sure Use ambient noise reduction isn't aggressively muting you.

3. CoreAudio Reset

If audio glitches occur, open Terminal on Mac and run sudo killall coreaudiod to safely restart the macOS audio service without rebooting.
